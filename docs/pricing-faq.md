# Pricing FAQ

Everything you need to know about OmniDeploy pricing.

## General Questions

### How does credit-based pricing work?

1 credit = 1 minute of compute time

Example:
- Running a model for 60 minutes = 60 credits
- Running 3 models for 20 minutes each = 60 credits

### Do credits expire?

No! Your credits never expire. Use them whenever you need.

### Can I upgrade/downgrade anytime?

Yes! You can change your plan at any time:
- **Upgrade**: Immediate, prorated billing
- **Downgrade**: Takes effect next billing cycle

## Free Tier

### What happens when I hit the limit?

On the Free tier, deployments pause when you hit 1,000 credits. They resume next month or when you upgrade.

### Is the Free tier really free forever?

Yes! No credit card required. No time limit. Use it as long as you want.

## Pro Tier

### What's the "grace period"?

Pro users get soft limits. If you exceed 5,000 credits:
1. We send alerts at 80%, 90%, 100%
2. Grace period of 500 extra credits
3. After grace, deployments throttle (not stop)

### Why ₹2,499/month?

We price in INR to serve Indian developers affordably. That's approximately $30/month - competitive with US-based alternatives.

## Enterprise Tier

### What's "metered invoice"?

Enterprise users get:
- 50,000 base credits included
- Overage at ₹0.80/credit (billed monthly)
- No hard limits - your services never stop

### Can I negotiate volume discounts?

Yes! Contact sales@omnideploy.info for custom pricing if you need:
- More than 100,000 credits/month
- Multi-year commitments
- Custom SLAs

## BYOC Pricing

### How does BYOC save money?

1. **30% bonus credits**: Pay for 1,000 credits, get 1,300
2. **Use your cloud discounts**: Reserved instances, savings plans
3. **No data transfer fees**: Data stays in your cloud

### Are there extra charges for BYOC?

No extra charges. You pay OmniDeploy + your cloud provider directly.

## Billing

### What payment methods do you accept?

- UPI
- Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
- Net Banking
- PayPal (International)
- Bank Transfer (Enterprise)

### Can I get a refund?

We offer refunds within 7 days of purchase if:
- You haven't used more than 10% of credits
- You contact support@omnideploy.info

### How do I cancel?

1. Go to Dashboard → Settings → Billing
2. Click "Cancel Subscription"
3. Your access continues until the end of billing period
